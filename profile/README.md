<p align="center"><b>Automatic chain of custody for every item in a move.</b></p>

---

Movers wear chest cameras. Boxterity records which item went into which box, and in what condition, without scanning anything. At delivery, it knows what came out of every box.

The same footage, labeled by the process itself, becomes real-world manipulation data for robotics labs.

*Box* + *dexterity*. Currently in proof of concept.
